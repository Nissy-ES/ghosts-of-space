WHY THIS EXISTS

Space history shouldn't feel like a textbook.

Young audiences usually meet space exploration through active missions. But thousands of engineering decisions, experiments and discoveries came from missions that are now silent.

Ghosts of Space turns those historical records into short, visual stories: what the hardware was built to do, what happened on the mission, what scientists learned, and what remained after the final signal.
